An easy Routine You can take inspo from:

6:00-6:30am - Wake up, Brush your teeth, wear something cozy

6:30-7:00am - Breakfast. (E.g. Eggs + Avocado + Pita Bread + A small square of Dark Chocolate)

What is so healthy about this breakfast?
  • Eggs has Choline to support the brain
  • Avocado has healthy fats like Omega-3
  • Pita Bread has the fiber + carbs you need
  • Dark chocolate has antioxidants and flavonols to support overall health

You do not need to eat this but it is important to eat something balanced

7:00am-8:00am Study Mathematics

8:00am-8:30am Take a break :D!

8:30am-9:30am Study Science

9:30am-10:00am Break Time! Snack on some healthy almonds and walnuts

10:00am-10:30am Start working on some light hobbies/ EC work.

10:30am-11:30am Study English

11:30am-12:30pm Lunch Time

12:30pm-1:30pm Study History

1:30pm-5:30pm Work on any pending assignments

5:30pm-6:30pm Dinner Time

6:30pm-8:00pm Read a Book and/or Excercise

8:00pm-8:30pm Take a quick shower

8:30pm Good night!

This routine does NOT work for everyone. It is meant to be a source of inspo for making your own schedule

High School Tips + Intro

Hi, My name is Faaezah, and I am currently a junior. I made this website because I really wished I had someone to help me figure out high school.

I will be your big sister, giving you plenty of tips and support.

Here is a pic of me mentoring computer science :D!

Anyways, here is some tips for 9th and 10th graders!

You are welcome!

9th Grade

    • In 9th grade, I think that you probably will have the most amount of free time.

    • I would say go ahead and explore different clubs so you can find something you are particularly interested in.

    • In my freshman year, I tried DECA, Robotics, and ML club.

    • One of the biggest regrets I have is that I did not explore as many clubs in my freshman year.

    10th Grade

    • In 10th grade, I think that this is one of the most overlooked year of high school.

    • Filter out clubs that you don't really like doing and start focusing on participation in competitions.

    • I left DECA my sophomore year as it was not for me. Then, I got really serious about robotics + comp sci

    • I participated in a hackathon and a lot of robotics competitions and those were really fun!

    • When you attend those competitions, the goal is not to win but rather to gain experience and see where you can improve your technical expertise.
