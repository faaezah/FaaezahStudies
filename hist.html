History tips

  1. Why does this matter?

    • AP World History is crucial to help you understand the factors that have influenced how the world is today.

    • It is good to know as it helps you also makes more informed decisions

    • We covered this so we can start with some motivation before we go deep into WHAP

  2. What should I do BEFORE the unit is taught?

    • You don't need to know the entire unit material before it is taught.

    • It is good, though to do some reading before-hand so you can have a little bit of context.

    • I highly suggest watching Heimler's history tutorials/ reading a textbook before the unit is there

    • Lowk this is very underrated! I wish more people knew about this!

  3. What should I do DURING the unit?

    • This honestly really depends on what kind of a note taker you are.

    • The best strategy that worked for me was to listen to the notes in class, and then take any notes at home
    • While the teacher was explaining, I used to jot down 2-3 things I did not understand/had questions on so I could cover it more in detail when I got home.
    • This is not a one-for-all method and it really depends on what YOU are good with.
    • That said, there is one key thing that all note-taking practices need: ACTIVE RECALL
  4. What is ACTIVE RECALL?

    • Active recall is an interactive way of studying

    • When studying, you don't just look over notes, but rather you pull information and test things DIRECTLY from your brain

    • Well, How can I do that?

    • Flashcards are an amazing way to do that.

    • If you are lazy like me, you can use Quizlet to practice.

  5. What about the FRQs?

    • There are two main different FRQs in WHAP: LEQs and SAQS

    • Both of these really reward following a specific pattern.

    • What I mean is there is a specific way the AP grader and your school wants you to write these.

    • To learn the structure, one should watch Heimler's and ask your instructor

    • There will be FRQs to which you don't know the answer but it is important to think about that time period and gather specific context that can help you with writing the FRQ

    • If you are still unstuck, move on and come back to it if you have time.

That was a lot! Good job for reading through all of it :)!
