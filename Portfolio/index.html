An easy Routine You can take inspo from:

6:00-6:30am - Wake up, Brush your teeth, wear something cozy

6:30-7:00am - Breakfast. E.g. (Eggs + Avocado + Pita Bread + A small square of Dark Chocolate)

What is so healthy about this breakfast?
  • Eggs has Choline to support the brain
  • Avocado has healthy fats like Omega-3
  • Pita Bread has the fiber + carbs you need
  • Dark chocolate has antioxidants and flavonols to support overall health

You do not need to eat this but it is important to eat something balanced

7:00am-8:00am Study Mathematics

8:00am-8:30am Take a break :D!

8:30am-9:30am Study Science

9:30am-10:00am Break Time! Nourish on some healthy almonds and walnuts

10:00am-10:30am Start working on some light hobbies/ EC work.

10:30am-11:30am Study English

11:30am-12:30pm Lunch Time

12:30pm-1:30pm Study History

1:30pm-5:30pm Work on any pending assignments

5:30pm-6:30pm Dinner Time

6:30pm-8:00pm Read a Book and/or Excercise

8:00pm-8:30pm Take a quick shower

8:30pm Good night!

This routine does NOT work for everyone. It is meant to be a source of inspo for making your own schedule
